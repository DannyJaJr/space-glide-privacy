Space Glide Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Space Glide (Android package com.innovatalux.spaceglide) is provided by Innovata Lux. This policy explains how information is handled in the Android game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your chosen glider name, game settings, difficulty, scores, journey progress, tutorial or introduction preferences, demo usage, and purchase-unlock status on your device. These records allow the game to remember your preferences and continue your progress. A glider name is a local profile label, not an online account; you can use a nickname.

The current game does not send these local gameplay records to a developer-operated server. Android may include app data in device backups depending on your device and backup settings.

Music you select

If you choose local music, the game accesses the audio files you select to play them and display their filenames in your playlist. The game does not upload those files to us. Removing a song from the playlist does not delete the original file from your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Google Play. The game receives purchase status and transaction information needed to enable or restore the purchased content. We do not receive your payment card details. Google processes purchase and related account information under its own policies.

Google Privacy Policy

Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The current game does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or share local gameplay records for advertising. The game communicates with Google Play as needed for store and purchase functionality.

Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until they are replaced or removed. You can clear the game's app data through Android Settings to remove locally stored records. Uninstalling also removes local app data, although device backups may retain or restore data according to your settings. Google Play purchase records are managed separately by Google; clearing game data does not cancel a purchase or delete Google's records.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of information you sent directly to us.

Children's privacy

The game does not require users to provide a real name, email address, or an online account. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game's local profiles. Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and support messages. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Security

Local game data relies on your device's security protections. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes
